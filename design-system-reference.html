2h Countdown Urgency States · Over an hour, Under an hour, Closing

Accessible multi-sensory signaling. Text switches alongside color ("Day ends in" → "Closing") so users who cannot distinguish amber from red still immediately recognize the final countdown phase.

Dark Theme

Mon, Sep 7, 5:35:44 PM Day ends in04:24:16
Under an hour
Mon, Sep 7, 10:12:03 PM Day ends in00:47:57
Under fifteen minutes
Mon, Sep 7, 10:52:40 PM Closing00:07:20

Colour is never the only signal — the label switches from “Day ends in” to “Closing”, so the state reads for anyone who can't separate amber from red. Numerals are tabular, so digits don't jitter as they tick.
