# Security audit (database surface of the release)

Method: catalog inspection of DEV (== `development` migrations) compared with PROD, the Supabase security advisors for both projects (GET, read-only), targeted reading of the helper/authorisation functions, and the existing test evidence (pgTAP 646 assertions; hosted DEV RLS/RPC suite 78 with real JWTs). **Not done:** a line-by-line review of all 5,156 migration lines — risk-driven sampling of the authorisation entry points only. Cross-tenant and privilege-escalation checks rely on the structural findings below plus the existing suites.

## Result: no blocker found

| Area                     | Finding                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| ------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |

Tenant and location are derived from the session row, never trusted from the caller. |
| Private helpers          | 38 new private helpers (`resolve_table_input`, `canonical_label_parts`, `displace_conflicting_entries`, `begin_board_write`, `tasks_begin`, …) are `REVOKE`d from `public, anon, authenticated`. | Past-day boards          | `assert_board_not_locked` makes any previous service day read-only and tip-finalized days locked.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |

## Advisors

- DEV: 34 × `authenticated_security_definer_function_executable` (WARN) — exactly the 34 new public RPCs listed above; no other function. 2 × `rls_enabled_no_policy` (INFO), 1 × `auth_leaked_password_protection` (WARN).
- PROD: 2 × `rls_enabled_no_policy` (INFO), 1 × `auth_leaked_password_protection` (WARN). The two INFO items and the leaked-password warning exist identically on both; the app signs users in with synthetic accounts and a PIN, so the leaked-password setting is not a release matter.
- The 34 WARNs are by design (browser-callable RPCs that authorise internally) and are recommended to be recorded as accepted, not "fixed" by moving the functions.

## Known, unchanged risk (not a blocker)

- **#106** — `private.has_org_role` still ORs `organizations.created_by`; a _deactivated_ organisation creator keeps privileged SELECT access platform-wide. Pre-existing in PROD, unchanged by this release; the new board mutation path (`begin_board_write`/`can_operate_board`) and Tasks (`tasks_*`) are membership-only and do not use the bypass. PROD has 1 organisation / 11 profiles, so exposure requires a deactivated creator; recommended to schedule #106 soon after the release.
- The two `rls_enabled_no_policy` INFO tables are deny-all by design (pre-existing, e.g. passcode tables).

## Not verified

- The hosted RLS suite was not re-run in this audit.
- Production Auth settings (JWT expiry, rate limits) were not inspected.
