Smoked Vanilla Old Fashioned

Signature Cocktails

Edit

Perfect

Reference Build

Ingredients

  • Bourbon2oz
  • Demerara syrup0.25oz
  • Vanilla syrup0.25oz
  • Aromatic bitters1dash
  • Walnut bitters1dash
Method
Stir
Garnish
Orange peel and cherry

Preparation

  1. Add all ingredients to mixing glass.
  2. Add ice.
  3. Stir until chilled and diluted.
  4. Strain over a large cube.
  5. Smoke if desired.

BARTENDER NOTE

Express the orange peel over the finished drink before garnishing.
